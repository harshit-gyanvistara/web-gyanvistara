# Gyanvistara Site Brief

## Audience
- **Teachers (primary):** overloaded with prep and paperwork; want ready-to-use, curriculum-aligned material fast.
- **Principals / admins (buyers):** want consistent quality, fewer staffing gaps, and visibility across classes.

## Pain points
- Hours lost weekly on lesson plans, worksheets and question papers.
- Differentiating for mixed-ability classes of 40+ is hard.
- Deadlines, tasks and events scattered across WhatsApp, diaries and registers.
- Admins juggle staff rosters and substitutions manually.
- Generic global AI tools ignore Indian boards, languages and context.

## Value props
1. Lesson plans, worksheets, question papers, activities, summaries in minutes.
2. Writing assistant for circulars, remarks and parent communication.
3. Built for Indian classrooms (CBSE/ICSE/state boards).
4. Calendar and tasks in one place.
5. Admin staff roster for principals.
6. Teacher stays in control: every output is editable.

## "Why you need us" (6 points)
Note: all numbers are ILLUSTRATIVE, not measured results; label "Illustrative" in the UI.
1. **Get your evenings back** - Draft a lesson plan in about 2 minutes instead of an hour (illustrative).
2. **Question papers, balanced** - Set papers by chapter, difficulty and marks, with answer keys.
3. **Made for India** - Aligned to your board, grade and syllabus, not a foreign template.
4. **Every child, every level** - Generate worksheet variants for support, core and stretch (illustrative: 3 levels).
5. **One calm dashboard** - Calendar, tasks and deadlines together, no more lost messages.
6. **Run the school smoothly** - Admins manage the staff roster and cover in one view.

## Workflow (5 steps)
1. **Pick your class** - Choose board, grade, subject and chapter.
2. **Tell us the goal** - Lesson plan, worksheet, paper, activity or summary; add a note if you like.
3. **AI drafts it** - A structured, curriculum-aware draft appears in seconds.
4. **Refine and personalise** - Edit, regenerate, adjust difficulty, use the writing assistant.
5. **Teach and track** - Export or print, schedule on the calendar, tick off tasks.
